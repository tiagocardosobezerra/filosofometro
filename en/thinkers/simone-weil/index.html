Who was Simone Weil

Simone Weil was a French philosopher who worked in factories to learn about working-class life and fought in the Spanish Civil War. After mystical experiences, she drew close to Christianity without ever being baptized. She died young in England, and her notebooks, collected after her death, revealed an original and intense thinker.

Core ideas

  • Pure attention to others is the highest form of love and of prayer
  • Human beings need roots in a community, a land, and a culture
  • Force reduces people to things, both those who suffer it and those who wield it

The tradition: Christian mystic

Christian mysticism seeks direct encounter with God through contemplation, attention, and surrender. Simone Weil lived this path joined to a strong social commitment.
