Who was Mário Ferreira dos Santos

A philosopher, translator, and editor from São Paulo state, born in Tietê, author of an immense body of work published in dozens of volumes. He sought to build a rigorous philosophy, demonstrated step by step like mathematics, uniting Greek, medieval, and modern traditions. Little known in his lifetime beyond his own circle, he was rediscovered and now has readers in Brazil.

Core ideas

  • Something exists, and absolute nothingness cannot exist: all philosophy starts from here
  • Philosophy can be demonstrated rigorously, as an apodictic science
  • Great traditions of thought can be brought together in a coherent synthesis

The tradition: Concrete philosophy

Concrete philosophy is the name Mário Ferreira dos Santos gave to his system: a metaphysics that starts from self-evident truths and deduces the rest with rigor. He is the creator of this line.
