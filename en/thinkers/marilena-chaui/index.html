Who was Marilena Chauí

A philosopher from São Paulo, professor emerita at the University of São Paulo and one of the world's leading experts on Spinoza's work. Her book Convite à Filosofia has shaped generations of Brazilian students. She is also an active public intellectual who writes about democracy, ideology, authoritarianism, and culture in Brazil.

Core ideas

  • Democracy is the permanent creation of new rights by society
  • Ideology hides social conflicts and makes domination seem natural
  • Brazilian society carries authoritarian traits inherited from its history

The tradition: Political Spinozism

Political Spinozism uses Spinoza's ideas about freedom, desire, and democracy to think about present-day politics. Chauí is the great Brazilian interpreter of this tradition.
