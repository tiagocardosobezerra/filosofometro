Who was Epictetus

Epictetus was born a slave in Phrygia and taken to Rome, where he studied philosophy and later won his freedom. Expelled from Rome, he founded a school in Nicopolis. He wrote nothing, but his student Arrian recorded his teachings, which focus on the difference between what is and is not up to us.

Core ideas

  • Some things are up to us and others are not, and wisdom is knowing how to tell them apart
  • It is not events that disturb us, but the judgments we make about them
  • True freedom is inner, and no master can take it away

The tradition: Stoic

A Stoic seeks serenity by living according to reason and accepting fate. Epictetus is the most direct and practical Stoic, focused on controlling our own judgments.

In the same tradition: Seneca, Marcus Aurelius
