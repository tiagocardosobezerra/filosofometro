Who was Bertrand Russell

Bertrand Russell was a British logician, mathematician, and philosopher, as well as a tireless activist. With Whitehead, he tried to ground mathematics in logic and helped create analytic philosophy. He was jailed for his pacifism, campaigned against nuclear weapons, and won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1950.

Core ideas

  • Logical analysis of language clarifies and dissolves philosophical problems
  • Mathematics can be derived from principles of logic
  • We should believe in proportion to the evidence, keeping a reasonable doubt

The tradition: Analytic philosophy

Analytic philosophy seeks clarity through logic and careful analysis of language and concepts, one problem at a time. Russell was one of its founders.

In the same school: Gottlob Frege, Wittgenstein, Karl Popper
