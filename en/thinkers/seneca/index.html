Who was Seneca

Born in Córdoba, in Hispania, Seneca was a writer, senator and adviser to Emperor Nero, who eventually ordered his death. In letters and essays, he spoke in practical terms about anger, grief, time and death. He is one of the most widely read Stoic authors today, precisely because he deals with everyday problems.

Core ideas

  • Life is not short: we are the ones who waste much of our time
  • We should prepare for adversity by imagining it before it happens
  • Wealth is not an evil, as long as the sage does not depend on it

The tradition: Stoic

A Stoic seeks to live according to reason and nature, accepting what is not up to us. Seneca represents Roman Stoicism turned toward practical life.

In the same tradition: Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius
