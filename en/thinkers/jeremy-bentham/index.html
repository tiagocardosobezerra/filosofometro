Who was Jeremy Bentham

A jurist and English reformer, Bentham wanted to transform laws and institutions based on a calculation of pleasure and pain. He defended ideas far ahead of his time, such as wider voting rights, an end to cruel punishments and consideration for animals. He asked that his body be preserved, and it is still on display at University College London.

Core ideas

  • The right is what produces the greatest happiness for the greatest number of people
  • Pleasure and pain are the only real guides to human behavior
  • The suffering of animals counts too, since they can feel pain

The tradition: Utilitarian

Utilitarianism judges actions and laws by their consequences: the best is what produces the most well-being overall, adding up the happiness of everyone affected. Bentham was the systematic founder of this current.

In the same school: John Stuart Mill, Henry Sidgwick, Peter Singer
