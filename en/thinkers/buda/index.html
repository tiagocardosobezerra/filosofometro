Who was Buddha

Siddhartha Gautama was a prince who, according to tradition, left his palace after discovering sickness, old age, and death. After years of searching and meditation, he attained awakening and began teaching a path to overcome suffering. His teachings gave rise to Buddhism, practiced today by millions of people.

Core ideas

  • Life involves suffering, and its main cause is attachment and craving
  • Freedom from suffering is possible by following the Noble Eightfold Path
  • Nothing is permanent, not even the self, which is a constantly changing flow

The tradition: Buddhist

Buddhism is the tradition born from the teachings of the Buddha, aimed at liberation from suffering through wisdom, ethics, and meditation. The Buddha is its founder.
