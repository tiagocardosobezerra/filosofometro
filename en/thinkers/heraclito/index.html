Who was Heraclitus

Heraclitus was an aristocrat of Ephesus famous for his enigmatic style, which earned him the nickname The Obscure. He taught that everything is in constant change and that the conflict between opposites keeps the world in balance. His short, provocative fragments inspired everyone from the Stoics to Hegel and Nietzsche.

Core ideas

  • Everything flows: reality is in permanent transformation
  • Opposites are united, and the tension between them creates the order of the world
  • There is a universal reason, the logos, that governs all things

The tradition: Presocratic

The Presocratics are the first Greek philosophers, before Socrates, who tried to explain nature without relying only on myths. Heraclitus is one of the most original of the group.
