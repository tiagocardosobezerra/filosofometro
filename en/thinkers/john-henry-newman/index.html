Who was John Henry Newman

An Anglican priest at Oxford and leader of the Oxford Movement, he became a Catholic in 1845, a decision that shook Victorian England. He explained how we can be certain without strict proof, defended a university that forms the whole person, and was made a cardinal and, in 2019, a saint.

Core ideas

  • Real certainty comes from many converging reasons, not from a single proof.
  • Conscience is the voice of God inside each person.
  • Education should form the whole mind, not only train for a job.

The tradition: Catholic theology

Catholic theology seeks to understand the faith through reason and tradition. Newman renewed it with his study of how doctrine develops and how people come to believe.

In the same school: John Calvin, Pascal, G. K. Chesterton, Simone Weil, C. S. Lewis
