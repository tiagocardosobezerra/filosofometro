Who was G. K. Chesterton

Gilbert Keith Chesterton was an English journalist, novelist, and essayist, famous for his humor and his paradoxes. He created the detective Father Brown and defended Christianity in books such as Orthodoxy, converting to Catholicism in 1922. He criticized both capitalism and socialism and influenced writers such as C. S. Lewis.

Core ideas

  • Tradition gives a voice to past generations, not only to the living
  • Before tearing down an institution, you must understand why it exists
  • Property should be widely distributed among families

The tradition: Christian traditionalist

A Christian traditionalist defends the heritage of Christian faith and customs as a guide against the excesses of modernity. Chesterton made that defense with humor, paradox, and common sense.
