Who was Alasdair MacIntyre

A Scottish philosopher who settled in the United States and died in 2025 at 96. In After Virtue, he diagnosed modern morality as a set of fragments with no possible agreement. He proposed recovering Aristotle's ethics of virtue, lived within communities and traditions that give meaning to our choices.

Core ideas

  • Modern moral debate is fragmented because it has lost a shared idea of the good
  • Virtues are learned in practices and communities with a shared history
  • We understand our lives as a narrative tied to a larger tradition

The tradition: Communitarian

Communitarianism criticizes liberal individualism and highlights the role of community and tradition in moral formation. MacIntyre is one of its central names, though he disliked the label.

In the same school: Hannah Arendt, Charles Taylor
