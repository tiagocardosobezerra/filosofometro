Who was Schopenhauer

Arthur Schopenhauer was a German philosopher of difficult temperament, an avowed rival of Hegel. He taught that behind the world lies a blind and insatiable Will, the source of constant desire and suffering. One of the first Westerners to study Buddhism and Hinduism, he influenced Nietzsche, Freud, Wagner and many writers.

Core ideas

  • Behind everything lies a blind Will that is never satisfied
  • Life swings between the pain of unfulfilled desire and boredom
  • Compassion for the suffering of others is the true basis of morality

The tradition: Pessimist

Philosophical pessimism is the view that existence brings more pain than joy and that the world has no good purpose. Schopenhauer is its great representative, proposing renunciation of desire as the way out.
