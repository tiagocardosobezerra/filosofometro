Who was Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius was emperor of Rome and spent much of his reign on military campaigns at the frontiers. In his spare hours, he wrote reflections to himself in Greek, which became known as the Meditations. The text shows a powerful man trying to be just, humble and serene, and it became a classic of practical wisdom.

Core ideas

  • We should accept with serenity what happens, for it is part of the order of the universe
  • All human beings are part of one rational community
  • Life is short, and each day should be lived with virtue and a sense of duty

The tradition: Stoic

A Stoic seeks virtue and inner peace by accepting what he cannot control. Marcus Aurelius shows Stoicism practiced by the man with the greatest power in the world.

In the same tradition: Seneca, Epictetus
