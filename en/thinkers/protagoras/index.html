Who was Protagoras

Protagoras was the most celebrated of the sophists, teachers who charged fees to teach rhetoric and the art of winning debates. A friend of Pericles, he became known for the idea that the human being is the measure of all things. Plato criticized him harshly, but he was a pioneer in thinking about truth and values from human experience.

Core ideas

  • The human being is the measure of all things: truth depends on who perceives it
  • About the gods it is impossible to know whether they exist or not
  • On any subject there are arguments on both sides, and both can be defended

The tradition: Sophist

Sophists were traveling teachers who taught oratory and politics, often doubting absolute truths. Protagoras is the most famous and the most respected of them.
