Who was Leibniz

Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz was a philosopher, mathematician, diplomat and librarian in the service of the court of Hanover. He invented infinitesimal calculus at the same time as Newton and imagined calculating machines. For him, the universe is made of simple units, the monads, and God chose to create the best of all possible worlds.

Core ideas

  • The real world is the best of all possible worlds
  • Reality is made of monads, simple and spiritual units
  • Nothing happens without a sufficient reason that explains why it is so

The tradition: Optimistic rationalist

An optimistic rationalist trusts reason to explain everything and believes the world is good. Leibniz is the example, with his thesis of the best of all possible worlds.
