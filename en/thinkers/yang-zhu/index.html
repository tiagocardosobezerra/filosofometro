Who was Yang Zhu

A Chinese thinker of the Warring States period, Yang Zhu became known for arguing that each person should preserve their own life and nature above all. He left no writings; his ideas have reached us through critics such as Mencius and through a chapter of the Liezi that extols the pleasures of a short life.

Core ideas

  • Caring for oneself and preserving one's life matters more than serving the State or seeking fame
  • Life is short and death erases everyone equally, so we should enjoy the present
  • No external gain, not even the whole world, justifies sacrificing one's own body

The tradition: Yangism

Yangism is the Chinese current that puts self-preservation and the enjoyment of life above social duties. Yang Zhu is its founder and the favorite target of the Confucians, who saw him as selfish.
