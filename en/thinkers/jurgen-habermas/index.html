Who was Jürgen Habermas

A German philosopher and sociologist, heir to the Frankfurt School, who died in March 2026 at 96. He devoted his life to thinking about how reason and dialogue can found a legitimate democracy. He was a constant public voice in postwar Germany, defending European integration and open debate among citizens.

Core ideas

  • Legitimate norms are those that everyone affected would accept in free dialogue
  • The public sphere is the space where citizens debate and keep power in check
  • Communicative action aims at mutual understanding, not just personal success

The tradition: Critical theory

Critical theory, born in the Frankfurt School, analyzes society to reveal domination and point toward paths of emancipation. Habermas is the leading name of its second generation.
