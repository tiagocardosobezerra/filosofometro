Who was Epicurus

Epicurus founded the Garden in Athens, a community open even to women and slaves. He taught that the goal of life is pleasure, understood as the absence of pain in the body and disturbance in the soul. Far from the image of parties and excess, he advocated a simple life with friends, free from fear of the gods or of death.

Core ideas

  • Happiness is the absence of pain in the body and disturbance in the soul
  • Death is nothing to us, since when it arrives we no longer exist
  • Simple pleasures and friendship are worth more than luxury and power

The tradition: Epicurean

An Epicurean follows Epicurus in pursuing a calm, moderate pleasure, based on an atomist view of the world. He is the founder and great reference of the school.

In the same school: Aristippus, Lucretius, Oscar Wilde, Michel Onfray
