Who was Michel Onfray

A popular and controversial French philosopher, author of dozens of books and founder of the Université Populaire de Caen, open to everyone. He proposes a philosophy tied to the body, pleasure, and concrete life, against what he sees as the ascetic legacy of Christianity. He rehabilitates forgotten thinkers of a materialist and hedonist tradition.

Core ideas

  • Pleasure is a good to be cultivated, as long as it harms no one else
  • Philosophy should start from the body and concrete life, not from abstractions
  • An open atheism frees morality from religious guilt and prohibitions

The tradition: Contemporary hedonist

Contemporary hedonism revives the old idea that pleasure is the central good of life, with ethics and care for others. Onfray is today its best-known defender.
