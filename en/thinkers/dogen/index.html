Who was Dōgen

A Japanese monk who traveled to China to deepen his studies and, on returning, founded the Sōtō school of Zen in Japan. He taught that seated meditation is not a means of reaching enlightenment but is already enlightenment in action. His work Shōbōgenzō is considered one of the most profound texts of Japanese philosophy.

Core ideas

  • Sitting in meditation is already enlightenment, not merely a path to it
  • Being and time are inseparable: each moment contains all of existence
  • Studying oneself leads to forgetting the self and opening to all things

The tradition: Zen Buddhist

Zen Buddhism is a branch of Buddhism that values meditation and direct experience above doctrine. Dōgen founded the Sōtō school and is one of its greatest masters.
