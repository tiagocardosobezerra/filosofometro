Who was Hegel

Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel was a professor in Jena, Heidelberg and Berlin, where he became the most influential philosopher in Germany. He built an enormous system in which history, art, religion and the state are stages in the development of Spirit. His dialectic inspired admirers and critics alike, from Marx to the existentialists.

Core ideas

  • Reality develops through conflicts and overcomings, in a dialectical movement
  • History is the path by which human freedom is realized
  • We recognize ourselves as subjects through the recognition of others

The tradition: German idealist

German idealism was the post-Kant current that saw reason or spirit as the basis of all reality. Hegel is its culmination, with a system that embraces nature, history and culture.

In the same school: Kant, Schopenhauer
