Who was Thomas Aquinas

Thomas Aquinas was an Italian Dominican friar who taught at the University of Paris. In the Summa Theologica, he united Aristotle's philosophy with Christian faith in a broad, organized system. Canonized and declared a Doctor of the Church, he remains the main philosophical reference of Catholicism today.

Core ideas

  • Reason and faith do not oppose each other, since both come from God
  • It is possible to demonstrate the existence of God through five rational ways
  • There is a natural law, accessible to reason, that guides moral good

The tradition: Scholastic

A Scholastic is a thinker of the medieval universities who used logic and debate to reconcile faith and reason. Thomas Aquinas is the high point of Scholasticism.
