Who is Byung-Chul Han

A philosopher born in South Korea who studied and taught in Germany. In short, widely read books such as The Burnout Society, he analyzes the exhaustion, hyperconnection, and pressure to perform of today's world. For him, we now exploit ourselves while believing we are free.

Core ideas

  • In the achievement society, each person exploits themselves believing they are free
  • An excess of positivity and stimuli produces exhaustion and depression
  • Total transparency and hyperconnection erode intimacy and silence

The tradition: Contemporary critic

Contemporary critic here means a thinker who diagnoses the ills of present-day society, such as fatigue, consumption, and digital surveillance. Han is today one of the most widely read in this role.

In the same school: Theodor Adorno, Herbert Marcuse, Jürgen Habermas
