Who was David Hume

A Scottish philosopher and historian, Hume was one of the sharpest minds of the Enlightenment. He took empiricism to its ultimate consequences and showed that many everyday certainties, like the idea of cause and effect, come from habit and not from pure reason. His skepticism woke Kant and still shapes debates about science, morality and religion.

Core ideas

  • All ideas are copies of impressions that arrived through the senses
  • We do not see causation: we expect the future to repeat the past out of sheer habit
  • Reason alone moves no one; it is passions and feelings that decide

The tradition: Empiricist

Empiricism says that all knowledge comes from experience, not from innate ideas. Hume is the most radical empiricist: he used this principle to show the limits of what reason can prove.
