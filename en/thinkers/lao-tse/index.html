Who was Laozi

A semi-legendary figure of ancient China, traditionally considered the author of the Tao Te Ching, a short, poetic book. According to tradition, he worked as an archivist at court and left for the west riding a water buffalo. Historians debate whether he really existed, but his ideas founded Taoism and still influence Eastern thought today.

Core ideas

  • The Tao is the natural way of all things and cannot be defined in words
  • Acting without forcing, wu wei, is more effective than fighting the flow
  • Simplicity, humility, and gentleness overcome rigidity and brute force

The tradition: Taoist

Taoism is the Chinese tradition that seeks to live in harmony with the Tao, the natural flow of the universe. Laozi is considered its founder, alongside Zhuangzi.
