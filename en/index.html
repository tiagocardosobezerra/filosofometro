The map

Eleven tensions that define a way of thinking

Each theme sets two legitimate answers to an old question against each other. There is no right side: there is your side, and who stood on it before you.

  1. Knowledge: Reason × Experience
  2. Will: Fate × Freedom
  3. Meaning: Given meaning × Created meaning
  4. Ethics: Duty × Consequences
  5. Social life: Individual × Community
  6. Happiness: Pleasure × Virtue
  7. Reality: Matter × Spirit
  8. Truth: Certainty × Doubt
  9. Change: Tradition × Rupture
  10. Suffering: Accept × Resist
  11. Power: Authority × Autonomy
The method

No black box

The whole calculation shows in your result: every answer, every theme, every distance. You can redo the math by hand.

  1. You answer

    Pick a level and agree or disagree with the statements. They come mixed up, and on each theme half of the statements pull each way.

  2. We map your shape

    Your answers become a position on each of the 11 themes. We compare the shape of that profile with that of 130 thinkers.

  3. You audit

    See step by step why you got who you got, compare with any thinker and download the data.
